## Decisions / follow-up

1. **Open Art Spaces is the only material schedule-date discrepancy found in this pass.** The existing five entries cannot all be reconciled with the organiser's council application without recovering the later date-specific announcements and licence outcomes.
2. Flower Market's official FAQ conflicts with its official calendar on the winter opening time (09:00 versus 09:30). The code follows the calendar and the dated October listing; ask the organiser to reconcile the FAQ.
3. Antiques, FoodSt recurrence and the Food Market Easter closure rely at least partly on local editorial listings rather than primary organiser date calendars. Treat these as supported but lower-confidence, and seek organiser confirmation where practical.
4. The Food Market's location is correctly represented as Pavilion, Market Drive, Chiswick W4 2RX. Its separate reference to the Grove Park Farm House farmyard is descriptive, not a conflicting postal location.
5. The 2027 schedule remains provisional. A recurring pattern is not confirmation of a particular 2027 event date, and one-off 2026 events must not be projected forward.

## Audit limitations

This pass checks the published evidence available on 27 November 2026. It does not prove that every past event took place exactly as advertised, nor does it replace confirmation from organisers where their pages conflict, are incomplete, or describe a proposed licence rather than an approved event. No runtime calendar data has been changed by this audit.
